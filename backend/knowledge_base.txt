🌍 Global Scope
NextGen Consultancy provides job placements across all countries in Europe as well as Israel.

📋 Standard Terms & Candidate Benefits
While specific contracts vary, the baseline conditions for candidates generally include:

Contract Length: Typically 2-year employment contracts.

Work Hours: Usually 8 to 9 hours a day, 6 days a week, with opportunities for overtime.

Living Essentials: Employers almost universally provide accommodation, and most provide either food/duty meals or transportation. Medical coverage is also a standard provision (e.g., in Belarus, medical is provided 3 days after arrival).

Leave: Annual leave and pay are provided as per applicable local laws.

👤 General Eligibility & Requirements
Age Limits: Generally ranges from 21 to 48 years old depending on the country (e.g., under 35 for Serbia, up to 48 for Russia, and 25-45 for standard Excel terms).

Language: Basic English speaking skills are preferred or required for almost all roles, with "compulsory" English needed for high-end roles like Cruise Liner Chefs.

Experience: A minimum of 2 years of experience is standard. Indian or Gulf experience with valid licenses is highly valued. (Note: Russia is an exception where freshers are explicitly allowed to apply).

Education: Basic roles often require a minimum of a 10th-pass certificate. Specialized roles require specific qualifications (e.g., Hotel Management degrees for Chefs).

Passport: Both ECR (Emigration Check Required) and ECNR passports are accepted.

📁 Strict Documentation Rules
For General/Construction Roles:

Passport (valid for at least 2.5 years).

Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) from the passport office.

Aadhar Card & Full-size photos.

Experience and Education certificates.

Medical fitness certificate (some countries require Embassy Visa Medicals; others just general medicals).

For Industry Roles (Heavier Scrutiny):

Passport (valid for at least 3 years).

Birth Certificate and Marriage Certificate (if applicable, government approved).

Specific Affidavits: Family/Spouse declarations and Industrial affidavits (Hebrew and English).

Global PCC Rule: If a candidate worked in any foreign country for more than 6 months, they need a PCC from that specific country, fully attested.

Israel-Specific: Candidates with spouses or parents in Israel are avoided. Siblings in Israel must be declared.

Skills Testing: Skilled workers must provide a video of their work along with their CV.

Interviews: Handled via Zoom; candidates must know how to use it (NextGen conducts mock tests to train them).
